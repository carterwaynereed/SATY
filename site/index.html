        SOURCED RULES · EXPLICIT EXPERIMENTS

        The playbook

        Independent research inspired by Saty Mahajan. No affiliation or endorsement.

        The decision sequence

        1. Map the day.

          Prior high/low, premarket range, opening range, previous completed daily ATR and scheduled catalysts.

        2. Read the direction.

          SATY: 8/21/34 ribbon on confirmed 3m and 10m bars for all four instruments. The 13/48 relationship, daily ATR trigger and session VWAP support that reading. VWAP stays neutral when the index feed has no traded volume.

        3. Wait for price to confirm.

          Desk v0.1: ribbon pullback, VWAP reclaim/rejection, or level break and retest. SATY engine: separately versioned trigger, Golden Gate, pullback, flip, re-entry and squeeze observations.

        4. Check the room.

          Desk paper model requires at least 1.5R to the nearest mapped target, fresh feed, calendar review and aligned trends. SATY reports each signal's actual target R and original stop.

        5. Record the outcome.

          Desk paper studies: next-minute simulated fill, underlying slippage, stop-first ties, 15-minute time exit. SATY target events: target before original stop in the same session. Never pool these outcomes.

        VWAP, scoring, multi-timeframe rules and risk filters are independent research hypotheses. SATY uses a frozen RTH variant; its 13/48 conviction differs from an extended-session implementation. Phase appears as context and does not silently modify the paper scanner.

        Source library

        Economic-event check

        The original scheduled paper monitor verifies official event calendars. Its review expires after four hours and blocks paper entries within ten minutes of listed high-impact events. SATY replay does not claim that calendar review.

        Calendar edits require the original local scanner. Hosted views read its saved review. SPX is never inferred from a multiplied SPY quote.

        Evidence before action

        Compare source, event, cohort, target, reward/risk and sample size before reading a hit rate. Unusual Whales context has its own timestamp, instrument and expiry coverage. A gamma wall is a provider estimate, not a guaranteed price barrier.
